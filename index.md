# Baraj Labs

Baraj Labs makes small, playful apps for Android.

## SingBack

Record your voice and hear it played backwards. Sing a song, play it in reverse, then try to sing the reversed version back. Trim your takes, save the ones you like and share them with friends.

Your recordings stay on your phone. SingBack has no accounts, no tracking and no servers.

- [Get SingBack on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.barajlabs.singback)
