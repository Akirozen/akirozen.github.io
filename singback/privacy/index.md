---
title: SingBack Privacy Policy
---

**Effective date:** 19 November 2026

SingBack ("the app") is made by Baraj Labs ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app on Android.

**In short:** SingBack does not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal data. Your recordings never leave your phone unless you choose to share them.

## Microphone and recordings

SingBack asks for access to your microphone so it can record your voice. Recording only happens while you are on the Record screen and have pressed Record.

Your recordings are processed entirely on your device: reversing, trimming, speed changes and playback all happen on your phone. Recordings are never uploaded to us or to anyone else.

You can refuse microphone access. The app will explain why it needs it, and you can turn it on or off at any time in your phone's settings.

## Files stored on your device

SingBack saves the following in its own private storage on your phone:

- the takes you save, as audio files
- a list of your takes (names, lengths, dates)
- your app settings (for example light or dark appearance and language)

This data stays on your device. Deleting a take removes its files. Uninstalling the app removes everything the app stored.

## Importing audio

If you import an audio file, you choose it yourself with your phone's file picker. SingBack only reads the file you pick and copies it into its own storage. It does not browse or read your other files, and imported files are never uploaded.

## Sharing and saving

When you tap **Share**, SingBack hands the audio file to the app you choose (for example a messaging app) through Android's share menu. What happens to the file after that is governed by that app's own privacy policy.

When you tap **Save to device**, SingBack saves the audio file to a "SingBack" album in your phone's media storage. It does not read your photos, videos or other media.

## Data we do not collect

SingBack does not:

- require an account or ask for your name, email or phone number
- collect your location, contacts, photos or other personal information
- use analytics, tracking or advertising SDKs
- send any data to our servers (we have none)
- sell or share any data with third parties

## Children

SingBack is intended for users aged 16 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy (for example, if we add advertising), we will update this policy before that version is released and change the effective date above.
